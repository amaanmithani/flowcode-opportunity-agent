# Search plans by channel

Research workers read this file. Each worker gets the general rules plus the
section for its own channel. `{brand}` is the brand name and `{year}` is the
current year.

## General rules (every worker)
- Read the brand's own sources first: newsroom, press page, investor relations.
- Only report things published or live in the **last 90 days**, or announced
  for the future. Skip anything older.
- Budget: your search limit depends on the channel (more where Flowcode's proof
  is strongest: packaging, TV, events), plus up to 3 full page reads. Use the
  searches. Read the 2-3 most promising pages in full and quote from them.
- For each finding, record: the claim, the exact URL, a **verbatim quote of
  12 to 40 words copied from the page**, and the publish date. A later code check
  fetches the URL and looks for that quote. A paraphrased quote gets dropped.
- Prefer primary sources: the brand's newsroom and press wires (PR Newswire,
  Business Wire), then trade press (Adweek, Ad Age, Marketing Dive, Food Dive,
  Packaging Dive, Retail Dive, Sports Business Journal).
- Do NOT use Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, X or LinkedIn as sources. They block
  automated reading. Use press coverage of social campaigns instead.
- If you find nothing current for your channel, say so. Do not pad the results.

## tv_broadcast — TV, streaming and broadcast
Queries:
- `{brand} new ad campaign` (also try the biggest sub-brands)
- `{brand} TV commercial {year}` or `site:ispot.tv {brand}`
- `{brand} holiday campaign ad`
- `{brand} Super Bowl {year+1}`
Sources: Adweek, Ad Age, Marketing Dive, Muse by Clio, iSpot (lists current national airings), the brand's newsroom.
Look for: new spots, tentpole ads, sponsored broadcasts, streaming or CTV buys.

## social_digital — social, creators and digital
Queries:
- `{brand} TikTok campaign`
- `{brand} influencer campaign {year}`
- `{brand} viral marketing`
Sources: marketing trade press about the campaign, never the social posts themselves.
Look for: creator programs, social-first launches, digital sweepstakes.

## email_loyalty — email, loyalty and first-party data
Queries:
- `{brand} rewards program`
- `{brand} loyalty app`
- `{brand} sign up exclusive offers`
Sources: the brand's own site (rewards, loyalty and sign-up pages), press releases.
Look for: loyalty programs, app launches, email or SMS acquisition, first-party data efforts.

## events — events, sponsorships and experiential
Queries:
- `{brand} sponsorship {year}`
- `{brand} official partner`
- `{brand} pop-up experience`
Sources: press wires, Sports Business Journal, the brand's sponsorship pages.
Look for: league or team deals, tours, festivals, pop-ups, sampling tours.

## packaging — packaging and product
Queries:
- `{brand} limited edition packaging`
- `{brand} new packaging {year}`
- `{brand} holiday packaging`
Sources: Packaging Dive, Packaging World, Food Dive, the brand's newsroom.
Look for: limited editions, redesigns, licensed or co-branded packs, on-pack promotions.

## retail — retail and in-store
Queries:
- `{brand} in-store display`
- `{brand} retail partnership`
- `{brand} exclusive at Walmart OR Target OR Costco`
Sources: Retail Dive, Grocery Dive, CStore Dive, press releases.
Look for: retailer exclusives, new distribution, in-store activations, displays.

## company_wide — campaigns, launches and initiatives
Queries:
- `{brand} announces`
- `{brand} launches`
- `{brand} partners with`
Sources: the newsroom, investor relations, press wires.
Look for: product launches, brand partnerships, new platforms, big marketing
initiatives that touch any of the channels above.
